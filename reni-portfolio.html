Field Notes

I'm Reni — a biochemistry student who's spent the past several weeks training as a virtual assistant, specializing in CRM and systems support. I like taking scattered, undefined problems and building something structured out of them: a form, a workflow, a pipeline, a checklist — whatever keeps nothing from falling through the cracks.

My background is in science, so I default to process: define the steps, test them, document what breaks, fix it, test again. That's exactly how the case study below came together.

Deal Record: Vantage Retail & Living

Role CRM Assistant
Tool Zoho CRM
Scope Built from scratch
Stage ● Complete
01 Lead Ingestion & Tagging
+
Built a live website inquiry form and custom fields so every lead is captured and tagged the moment it arrives — by source, deal size, and industry. WhatsApp and Social DM channels were modeled with tagged test leads, documented for live integration via Zoho SalesIQ and Zoho Social. Webforms · Custom Fields
02 Data Hygiene & Enrichment
+
Saved views catch leads with missing contact info and leads gone quiet for 14+ days, backed by a documented weekly manual dedup routine — built around the free-tier's tool limits, not in spite of them. Custom Views
03 Cadence Architecture
+
A workflow rule fires an instant welcome email and auto-assigns a two-day follow-up task the moment a lead is created — so every inbound message gets both an immediate response and a human check-in. Workflow Rules
04 Audience Segmentation
+
Saved views group leads by deal size, channel, and industry — so outreach can be targeted instead of blanket, and high-value leads never get lost in general volume. Custom Views
05 Pipeline Velocity Reporting
+
A live report groups every lead by pipeline stage, showing exactly how many sit at each point from first contact to close — the kind of visibility a founder needs without asking for it. Reports
06 Audit Reconciliation
+
A weekly checklist cross-checks CRM records against real interactions and payments — activity logs, stalled leads, converted deals, matching invoices — catching gaps before they compound. Documented Process
✓ Verified end to end: a real lead was submitted through the live web form and traced through capture → tagging → automation → reporting, confirming the full system works together, not just in pieces.
